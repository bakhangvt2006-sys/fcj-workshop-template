This page summarizes my **worklog** throughout my internship at First Cloud AI Journey. I completed the program in **11 weeks**, from **14/09/2026 to 27/11/2026**.

During this time, I started with the fundamentals of AWS and then gradually learned services for networking, compute, storage, databases, serverless, messaging, security, and AI. After every few weeks, I set aside one week to review and combine the services I had learned into a small architecture.

**Week 1:** [Getting familiar with AWS, the Console \& CLI, creating a Free Tier account, basic hands-on labs, and learning about VPC](1.1-week1/)

**Week 2:** [Learning and practicing IAM, Amazon S3, and Amazon EC2](1.2-week2/)

**Week 3:** [Learning and practicing Amazon RDS, Amazon DynamoDB, and Amazon Route 53](1.3-week3/)

**Week 4:** [Learning and practicing Amazon CloudFront, Amazon CloudWatch, and AWS CloudTrail](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5:** [Reviewing the services from weeks 1 to 4 and building a small architecture on AWS](1.5-week5/)

**Week 6:** [Learning and practicing AWS Lambda and Amazon API Gateway](1.6-week6/)

**Week 7:** [Learning and practicing Amazon SNS, Amazon SQS, and Amazon EventBridge](1.7-week7/)

**Week 8:** [Reviewing weeks 6 and 7 and building an event-driven architecture](1.8-week8/)

**Week 9:** [Learning and practicing ELB, EC2 Auto Scaling, AWS KMS, and AWS Secrets Manager](1.9-week9/)

**Week 10:** [Learning and practicing Amazon Bedrock, Amazon SES, and AWS WAF](1.10-week10/)

**Week 11:** [Reviewing weeks 9 and 10 and building an architecture with an ALB, an Auto Scaling group, and WAF](1.11-week11/)
