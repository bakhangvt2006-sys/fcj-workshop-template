### General Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
I found the environment at FCAJ friendly and comfortable. The team members were always ready to answer my questions whenever I ran into difficulties, and they often supported me even outside working hours.

**2. Support from Mentors / Admin Team**  
My mentors guided me very thoroughly. Whenever something was unclear, they explained it step by step and always encouraged me to ask more questions. The admin team provided complete and detailed learning materials.

**3. Relevance of the Work to My Major**  
The work I took on was closely related to what I studied at university, while also introducing me to new areas such as cloud computing and AI on AWS. This allowed me to review my foundational knowledge and gain practical working experience at the same time.

**4. Learning Opportunities \& Skill Development**  
During the internship, I was introduced to many new tools and skills, such as project management, teamwork, writing technical documentation, and reporting progress regularly. The practical experience my mentors shared also helped me see more clearly the career path I want to follow.

**5. Culture \& Team Spirit**  
Everyone here is respectful, takes their work seriously, and keeps a cheerful atmosphere. People are always willing to help each other when a team runs into difficulties in a project.

**6. Policies / Benefits for Interns**  
The program offers flexible scheduling: I could register in advance for the days I wanted to come to the office, and people were always ready to help whenever issues came up. I also had the chance to join workshops, knowledge-sharing events, and weekend meet-ups.

### Other Questions

* What I was most satisfied with was the mentors and the workspace. The mentors always did their best to answer everyone's questions and were ready to help as much as they could whenever people needed it.
* I think the company is already doing very well and doesn't need much improvement.
* If a friend asked me, I would recommend interning here because of the working environment and the people. It is also a chance to get hands-on with AWS services, learn more, and grow personally.
